# Third-party notices and original asset provenance

No third-party image, font, recording, or other media asset is shipped. The `assets` list in `public/assets/asset-manifest.json` is intentionally empty. Original procedural artwork, SVG icons, city-card illustrations, synthesized music and effects are inventoried in `originals` with their source files and creator/provenance. These are original project work; no project-code license is inferred or added.

No photographs, supplied reference screenshots, external fonts, hotlinks, or runtime asset downloads are used. The referenced Wikimedia files have **not** been downloaded, cleared or shipped. Original simplified landmark silhouettes are not geographical reconstructions.
